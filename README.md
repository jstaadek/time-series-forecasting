# time-series-forecasting — forecasting and plotting experiments

Learning work on time-series forecasting and Plotly, kept whole as one archive. Frozen:
nothing here is polished after the fact.

- `data/` — three classic public teaching datasets: daily total female births, monthly
  car sales, shampoo sales (CSV).
- `plotly-experiments/` — six scripts from June 2022 on building Plotly figures as
  dictionaries and graph objects, plus one generated HTML export. Folded in from the
  `cs50` repo on 2026-10-04, where they never belonged.

Datasets belong to their original publishers and are not licensed here; the scripts are
my own practice work.
